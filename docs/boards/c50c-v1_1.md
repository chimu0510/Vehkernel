# C50C V1.1

The C50C V1.1 is an STM32F407VET6 robot controller with an ICM20948 IMU, four motor drivers with encoders, an OLED and a USB gamepad port, and no RS-485. It is Vehkernel's first development board. Its hardware facts are in [`boards/c50c-v1_1/board.toml`](../../boards/c50c-v1_1/board.toml), and why the board needs what it needs is in [ADR-0024](../adr/0024-c50c-v1_1-board.md).

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Preset | `c50c-v1_1`, and `c50c-v1_1-bringup` for the bring-up image ([Building](../guide/building.md)) |
| Board ID | 22083 |
| Bootloader | Vehkernel bootloader in S0, on SERIAL2 and SERIAL1 at 115200 8N1 (spec 0008); installed with `flash_serial.py new-board` ([Flashing](../guide/flashing.md)) |
| Flash layout | S0 bootloader, S1–S2 parameters, S3 event log, S4–S7 firmware (ADR-0020) |

## SWD

Connect an ST-Link to header HDR2: pin 4 GND, pin 6 PA13/SWDIO, pin 8 PA14/SWCLK. Pin 2 is 3.3 V for the probe's voltage sense only; the board stays on its own supply. Flashing over SWD is in [Flashing](../guide/flashing.md#over-swd). The hardware watchdog stops while the probe halts the core, and after a fault the firmware stops at a breakpoint while a debugger is attached, with every actuator output off (ADR-0005).

The STM32 built-in USB DFU is not practical on this board, because PA11/PA12 go to the USB gamepad port, not to a Type-C port.

## Serial ports

| Port | Connector | UART | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| SERIAL1 | Type-C port | USART3, through a CH9102F | Link, `SERIAL1_BAUD` |
| SERIAL2 | One-key download port | USART1, through its own CH9102F | Link, `SERIAL2_BAUD`; download circuit |

Both Links are 8N1 with DMA both ways, at 921600 baud by default, so one cable both flashes and connects. Each adapter appears on the host as a serial device, `/dev/ttyACM0` or `/dev/ttyACM1` in the order the cables were plugged in, so the numbers change when a cable is replugged. Both adapters also show up in `/dev/serial/by-id/` under one name, `usb-WCH.CN_USB_Single_Serial_0002-if00`, so tell the ports apart by the Link instead: `link_monitor.py --check` reports the connected port as `mcu_link instance=0` on SERIAL1 and `mcu_link instance=1` on SERIAL2 ([Link monitor](../guide/link-monitor.md#connecting)).

On the download port the adapter's RTS drives BOOT0, and its DTR, with RTS, drives NRST, so the STM32 system bootloader is entered and left without touching the board ([Flashing](../guide/flashing.md#a-new-board-new-board)). `new-board`, `backup` and `reset` therefore need the SERIAL2 device; `update` works on either port. The bootloader's boot window adds about 1 s to a start after power-on or a reset (measured: 2.4 s from `flash_serial.py reset` to a Link handshake, against 1.4 s after a plain `reboot`, which skips the window)..

## IMU

The ICM20948 runs on software I2C, since its lines are wired the reverse of hardware I2C1 (ADR-0015), and publishes `imu_sample` at 200 Hz. Its mounting rotation is `YAW_90`, measured with the board's front edge forward ([Attitude](../guide/link-monitor.md#attitude)).

## Actuator power sense

`PE4` reads the drive power circuit switched on connector CN6: arming needs it on for 0.5 s, and losing it disarms ([Driving the wheels](../guide/driving.md#actuator-power)). It reads high while the drive supply is connected and low when it is not (measured at the hardware check of spec 0006).

## Local display and user key

The display is the SSD1306 OLED on the 6-pin connector (U11 or J4), clocked by software SPI on `PD7` (clock), `PD6` (data), `PD5` (reset) and `PD4` (data/command) with each half clock period at least 100 ns. The user key is S3 on `PC5`, which reads low while pressed through the internal pull-up.

The driver holds the OLED in reset for 100 ms after boot, then initialises it. Nothing reads the display back, so a board with the OLED unplugged boots, verifies and arms as usual. Both cost about 12 KB of Flash and 3 KB of RAM. The display's work item runs every 50 ms on `wq:slow` and busy-waits only while it writes changed pages, up to about 5.5 ms for a full screen such as a page switch and about 0.3 ms on average; the timing monitor shows its cost as `local_display`. Using them is in [Local display](../guide/local-display.md).

## USB gamepad

The USB gamepad port, CN8, is the STM32's USB OTG FS core on `PA11` and `PA12`, run as a host by TinyUSB (spec 0010, Kconfig `RC_USB_GAMEPAD`, on by default for this board). Its VBUS comes straight from the board's 5 V rail; nothing switches it. An XInput pad works whatever its vendor and product IDs: the Flydigi pad on hand, in its Xbox 360 mode, enumerates as `045E:028E`, and every stick axis, both triggers and every button pressed reached `manual_control_input` at the bench check of #124, the right stick's x positive to the right and the left stick's y positive forward. The gamepad's state goes out 50 times a second while it is connected ([Remote control](../guide/driving.md#remote-control)).
